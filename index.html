About Us

It started with one very special cat.

Cola City Cats grew from our family's love of Bengal and Savannah-type cats and their unique combination of intelligence, energy and personality.

Our kittens are raised indoors with regular handling and interaction. They grow up around children and a dog, helping them become comfortable with the sights, sounds and routines of a busy family home.

Frequently Asked Questions

Good questions make good matches.

If you don't see your question here, just ask. We're happy to talk about our kittens and what to expect.

Where are Cola City Cats located?

We are located in the Columbia, South Carolina area. Exact visit details are shared when arranging an appointment.

How are the kittens raised?

Our kittens are raised indoors with daily human interaction and are accustomed to children and a dog.

Are the kittens litter trained?

Yes. Our kittens are introduced to litter boxes and are off to a great start before going to their new homes.

Can I visit a kitten?

Visits are by appointment. Contact us and tell us which kitten you're interested in.
